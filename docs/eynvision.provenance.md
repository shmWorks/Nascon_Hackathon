## Unresolved and excluded

- NaSCon'26 sponsor/judge roster, named judges and scoring weights: not independently located. Local brief attributes the problem to Eynvision; sponsorship/judging also supplied by user. Search absence does not disprove the event relationship.
- Internal Eynvision news-reading need, buying intent and procurement requirements: no evidence found. Sponsor fit does not require an internal company tool; the brief targets readers.
- Ascent AI: linked by Eynvision; opening `joinascent.ai` returned a research-browser internal error. Functionality and availability unverified.
- Precise performance metrics excluded: Leo combines 80% time reduction with 4 hours to 20 minutes; these imply different reductions if measuring the same task. Animated counters sometimes retrieve as zero.
- Search results containing data-broker personal contact records excluded. No private data collected.
- No source found mandates MCP, LangGraph, agent orchestration, a specific model, enterprise integrations, or an Eynvision-only audience.

Company-controlled websites and LinkedIn repeat one interested publisher. No quantified sponsor performance claim is used in the recommendation. A team member's YC-backed employment does not establish that Eynvision is YC-backed.

## Research coverage and limits

- Research branches: company/portfolio, public professional team context, NaSCon relationship/rubric, immediate news-reading substitutes, and implementation implications. Four search-query batches in the main investigation and four in the delegated branch; follow-up page reads targeted disputed claims. Stop condition: decision-relevant claims supported or explicitly unknown, no unresolved blocking review finding.
- Searches included Eynvision + NaSCon/2026/judge/sponsor, personal morning paper, founders, official domain, and direct competitor documentation. Search absence is not proof of absence.
- Root causes of delivery risks are recommendations derived from the brief and reviewed product mechanics; research does not establish causal explanations of sponsor success or private judge psychology.
- Breadth is deliberately limited to immediate substitutes. An Inoreader page and Eynvision `/products` returned tool errors; no absence-of-feature conclusions were drawn. Ascent was not functionally tested. Several guessed case-study paths failed; P23 was subsequently reached through the official index. Tool access failure does not establish site downtime.
- The invoked Feynman deep-research skill references a missing `prompts/deepresearch.md`; exact-name search across both skill roots found no copy. Its available cited-brief/provenance instructions were combined with the complete invoked deep-research, research, adversarial-review and writing-for-agents procedures.

## Consolidated adversarial findings

Independent source, architecture and reader reviews were cross-examined; disputed severity was resolved after revision. Findings below describe corrected document risks, not observed application defects.

| Severity | Root cause and evidence | Resolution in the brief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | Separate partner domains were treated as independent corroboration. P1/P18 establish publication, not control independence. | Label as published claims; remove independent-validation implication. |
| Medium | Daily scheduling, fresh retrieval and elapsed-day operation were conflated as a same-day proof expectation. Deadline precedes tomorrow. | Separate the three checks; mark actual unattended next-day operation unverified. |
| Medium | Ordinary skip was assigned durable preference meaning absent reader intent. Brief separates skip and learning. | Record skip independently; use explicit preference feedback for adaptation proof. |
| Medium | Internal ranking change could pass without a changed paper. R4/A10 concern future editions. | Save and compare editions on controlled candidates; distinguish adaptation from improved value. |
| Medium | Requirement packaging and sponsor similarity substituted for desirability evidence. | State reader-value hypothesis; test a concrete sourced takeaway; avoid superiority/internal-demand claims. |
| Medium | Evidence presentation grew before source/PDF viability was established. | Dependency gate and core slice first; detailed metrics/failure evidence can live outside Loom. |
| Low | Document-supported competitor features were called verified without saying how. | Mark documented, not exercised; retain plan/access caveat. |

Residual uncertainty: actual judging weights, named NaSCon'26 judges, internal sponsor demand, comparative reader value and all unbuilt application behavior. None is represented as established.
