**Build a personal morning paper**

## The problem

News is scattered. The articles that matter to a person are spread across dozens of websites, apps and newsletters, and there is no single place to read them. Social media has that place: Instagram brings everything into one feed. The news has nothing like it.
So people jump from site to site, click through link after link, and still can't name three things they learned.

## The idea

Build a product that understands what each reader cares about, gathers the articles that matter to them from across the web every day, and gives them one personal morning paper. The reader reads it on your web page, clicks through to any original article, skips what they don't want, or downloads the whole paper as a PDF.

## What to build

Each part below says what the product should cover. How it looks and works is up to you.

### 1. Onboarding experience

Design an onboarding experience that captures enough about the user's interests to personalize their paper, while maintaining a great user experience.

### 2. PDF

Create a daily paper PDF that is made fresh every day and feels inspired by the structure and layout of a traditional newspaper.
The paper should be as personalised to the reader as possible, using what the product understands about them to decide what content is most relevant and how it should be presented.

### 3. Webpage

The daily paper should be available as a complete reading experience on the product's webpage, with the design and interaction left up to you.
The same edition should also be available as a downloadable PDF inspired by a traditional newspaper as mentioned above.
Readers should be able to access their previous papers as well.

### 4. Learning

Over time, the product should become better at understanding the reader's interests and use that understanding to improve future papers.
How you capture and interpret those signals is up to you.
The goal is: the more the reader uses the product, the better the paper should become at reflecting what they actually care about.

### 5. Room to Explore

The requirements above describe the core product, not the limits of it.
You are encouraged to interpret what a personal daily paper could become.
It may include ideas beyond traditional news, introduce new ways of discovering information, use AI in unexpected ways, or create experiences we have not specified here.
